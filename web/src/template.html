    HOW TO PLAY

    Build the maze. Make the courier walk.

    A courier always takes the shortest route from the cottage to the tower, visiting numbered wells in order. Longer route, bigger score. Press Walk to watch him go.

    Tap a tile to build a wall. Each wall costs 1 energy. Tap it again to take it back and get the energy back.

    Rocks are natural walls. Clearing one costs more energy, shown on the puzzle chip. You can put it back for a refund.

    Gold zones multiply every step taken inside them. Bend the route through them.

    He slips diagonally between blocks that only touch at a corner, so join walls edge to edge. You can never seal him in: a move that blocks the route flashes red and is undone.

    Hit Submit to post your score. Everyone in the world gets the same daily maze, so bring friends.
